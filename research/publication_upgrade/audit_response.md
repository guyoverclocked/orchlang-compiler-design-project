The strongest new result is engineering evidence: prefix sharing resolves the
previously unexplained 128-call cap under the same default limits and improves
replay work in the controlled family, while the 256-call case remains UNKNOWN.
The precise same-machine timing result is in the frozen paired JSONs and new
manuscript; it is not inferred from the uploaded audit's one-shot timings.

Primary research also identifies closer 2026 agent IFC and instrumentation
work, including APPA, FLOWSEAL and ProMCP. These make a blanket first-agent-
privacy or first-resource-analysis claim untenable. OrchLang's usage observer
does not cover provider cache timing, transport sizes, persistent stores,
arbitrary effects or output content. Equal laws under a declared kernel do
not validate that kernel against a provider.

The zero-admission external result is retained even though it weakens the
initial publication narrative. The current software is best presented as an
offline request/usage-model verification tool with narrow source adapters.
Claiming a scientific breakthrough, broad framework coverage, a verified whole
compiler, independent human artifact evaluation or journal acceptance would
exceed the completed evidence.
