## Actionable wording corrections

1. **Identify the decisive exact-distance fixture as a fixed-kernel witness.** The main paper reports exact TV `1/2` against conservative bound `1` at lines 81–89, and the supplement repeats the result at lines 153–156. The fixture `research/scp_strengthening/exact_uncertain_distance/examples/unequal_prefix_mass.json` has six history rows, each with one generator. Thus every row hull is a singleton: this example demonstrates a strict benefit over the pathwise bound on a fixed supplied kernel, not a case with nontrivial row uncertainty. The result is correct, and the general prototype accepts non-singleton row hulls; add a short parenthetical such as “the separating fixture uses singleton row sets” so readers do not mistake the illustration for evidence about a nontrivial uncertainty set. The name `unequal_prefix_mass_decisive` does not change the singleton fact.

2. **Label the holdout rejection counts as first-rejection reasons.** The supplement says at line 217 that candidates are “six at unsupported imports and three at top-level imports.” The frozen result records these under `first_rejection_counts`, so these numbers do not show that each file has only that incompatibility or exhaustively characterize its unsupported behavior. Suggested wording: “The first rejection was an unsupported import for six candidates and a top-level `Import` for three.” The denominator and outcome remain correct: zero admitted whole files among nine AST graph candidates.

3. **Make the source-capsule execution boundary visible in the main-paper summary.** The supplement is precise at lines 192–212: selected hash-pinned source segments were compiled/executed with actual pinned LangGraph under finite offline doubles; the whole modules were not imported, and final reproduction replays recorded finite models rather than rerunning original source. The main article calls them “bounded public-source slices” and says they use mocks, but does not explicitly name actual pinned-runtime execution. A compact clause in the main evaluation table or nearby prose would prevent readers from confusing source execution, whole-file admission, and subsequent model replay. Preserve the existing six/12 authored schedule counts and no-provider/no-whole-file boundary.
