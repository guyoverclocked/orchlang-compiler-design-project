# Wider journal search, 24 October 2026

Author requested alternatives to SCP. This is a venue-fit assessment, not an
acceptance prediction, indexing certification, or change to experimental claims.
No submission, paid service, release, or author contact was performed. API spend
remains USD 0. Existing manuscript and tested release archives are unchanged.

## Shortlist and evidence

1. **Journal of Open Research Software (JORS), Software Metapaper.** Best match
   if the contribution is reusable checking/reproduction infrastructure. Its
   review explicitly checks software behavior, quality control, documentation,
   limitations and concrete reuse. Requires an openly licensed public deposit
   and its template; the current SCP manuscript needs adaptation. This route
   does not certify broad theory novelty. APC required; waiver request may be
   made in the cover letter. Exact current amount not established here.
   [Submission guide](https://openresearchsoftware.metajnl.com/about/submissions),
   [review criteria](https://openresearchsoftware.metajnl.com/about/editorialpolicies),
   [APC policy](https://openresearchsoftware.metajnl.com/about).
2. **SoftwareX.** Publisher describes detailed research-software and application
   articles. Plausible artifact-centered alternative, conditional on a convincing
   demonstration of usefulness. Open access; current APC, article-specific guide
   and template still need direct verification. ScienceDirect fetch returned
   403; publisher's Research Elements overview was accessible. Do not substitute
   inconsistent third-party prices or journal-clone domains for the guide.
   [Publisher overview](https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/tools-and-resources/research-elements-journals).
3. **SCP Original Software Publication.** Retain as a direct topical match.
   Existing checklist and official template verified on 23 October remain the
   starting point. Stronger application/innovation evidence is still needed.
4. **Software Impacts.** Publisher describes short articles on impactful,
   reusable software applied to research challenges. We have no external
   adoption evidence; speculative impact cannot fill that gap. Open access;
   current APC and detailed guide unverified. Same publisher overview above;
   [official template](https://legacyfileshare.elsevier.com/promis_misc/SIMPAC_Article_Template.pdf)
   explicitly requires public code and its template (dated July 2021, not a
   replacement for checking the current guide).
5. **STTT.** Strong subject fit for semantics-based verification tools, but its
   scope explicitly emphasizes realistic systems and broader tool chains. A
   stronger end-to-end case study is needed; it is not an easier substitute for
   SCP. Hybrid journal; subscription publication has no APC.
   [Scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/10009/aims-and-scope),
   [fees](https://link.springer.com/journal/10009/how-to-publish-with-us).
6. **Software Quality Journal.** Accepts techniques/case studies including formal
   methods and quality assurance. Would need an evaluation centered on measured
   assurance value and comparisons. Hybrid; subscription route has no APC.
   [Scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/11219/aims-and-scope),
   [fees](https://link.springer.com/journal/11219/how-to-publish-with-us).
7. **Software Testing, Verification and Reliability.** Relevant to conformance,
   mutation and differential validation, but would need a generalizable testing
   contribution and stronger application evidence.
   [Publisher scope](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/10991689).
8. **The Art, Science, and Engineering of Programming.** Accepts programming
   languages, compilers, verification and engineering work, but its engineering
   criteria require results clearly better than an accepted baseline. We have
   not established that advantage. No publication APC; authors are expected to
   present at a partnering conference, which may add costs. Batch review targets
   four months; not a one-week acceptance route. The site banner still showed
   October 1, 2026; do not call that an upcoming deadline on October 24.
   [Criteria](https://programming-journal.org/cfp/),
   [operation and fees](https://programming-journal.org/purpose/).
9. **Software: Practice and Experience.** Languages/compilers and experience
   reports are in scope, but the scope excludes work focused on verification
   beyond design/implementation. A practical compiler-design/experience paper
   would require substantial reframing and realistic use. Not preferred for the
   present verification-centered narrative.
   [Scope](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/1097024x/homepage/productinformation.html).
10. **Journal of Systems and Software.** Broad tools/verification/SE-for-AI scope;
    all claims require evidence and new SE knowledge. New Ideas and Trends is an
    actual category, but still needs a specific new idea; existing generic
    relational-cost claims do not establish it. In Practice requires real
    industrial experience that is not currently present. Not an easier fallback.
    [Publisher scope and tracks](https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/journal-of-systems-and-software/0164-1212).
11. **JOSS.** Zero publication fees, but the current submission guide requires
    more than six months of public development spanning that period and
    demonstrated research use. Public history/eligibility has not been
    established for this project; do not promise a near-term submission based
    on code quality alone. A burst of commits does not meet the gate. Human
    design contributions and AI disclosure also matter.
    [Current gates](https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submitting.html),
    [fees and scope](https://joss.theoj.org/about).

PeerJ Computer Science was screened, but its current detailed scope/fee pages
were inaccessible through the fetch tool. Historical marketing and third-party
prices are insufficient for a current recommendation; leave detailed evaluation
open rather than assign false precision.

## Decision

For the achieved artifact, investigate JORS and SoftwareX alongside SCP OSP.
STTT becomes more persuasive after realistic integrations. No target fixes the
existing usefulness/novelty gap automatically. Near-term effort should prioritize
faithful end-to-end cases and clean installation/reuse, then a venue-specific
manuscript. Current heuristic readiness: JORS 6/10, SoftwareX 6/10, SCP OSP 5/10;
these are subjective judgments, not percentages or editorial predictions.

The USD 5 cap concerns API testing, not publication charges. No publication
funding has been established. Confirm the exact APC, taxes, waiver and any VIT
agreement before selecting a fee-bearing route. Do not assume library access
covers an APC. No verified indexing/quartile claims are made in this search.
Submission within a week and journal acceptance within a week are different
milestones; no reviewed source supports promising the latter.
