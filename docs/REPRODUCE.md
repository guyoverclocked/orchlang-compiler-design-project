# Reproduction

The current reproducibility instructions are maintained in `publication/README.md`.
They distinguish historical observations, new completion runs, core proof checking,
independent operational comparison, model-dependent tests and the retained disk
scientific implementation. Raw provenance and exact environment versions are in
`publication/evidence`.

The historical Linux/Python 3.13 campaign and new Windows/Python 3.12 campaign have
separate timing and implementation identities. Do not merge their latency results
or describe historical executions as runs of the combined runtime.
